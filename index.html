About RisCalc

RisCalc is a comprehensive risk calculation and modeling application built for traders, investors, and risk managers. Our platform helps you quantify financial exposure, calculate optimal position sizing based on custom risk parameters, and simulate market volatility.

By providing immediate access to key statistical risk metrics (such as Value at Risk, Drawdown models, and Sharpe ratios), RisCalc assists you in making data-driven decisions to preserve capital and optimize portfolio performance.

How We Use Google Services

RisCalc integrates with Google services to streamline your workflow:

  • Google Sign-In: We use Google Identity Services to allow you to securely log in to your RisCalc account without needing to maintain separate passwords.
  • Data Sync (If applicable): We securely access only the specific scopes you authorize to allow you to save and export your risk reports directly into your personal files.

RisCalc does not access, store, or share your personal private data outside of the explicit features required to run this service. We strictly adhere to Google API Services User Data Policies.
