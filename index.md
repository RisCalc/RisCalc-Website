## Key Features

### Institutional Position Sizing
Stop guessing your lot sizes. Use our real-time calculator to define your risk in cash or percentage. Our dynamic "Stake per Pip" slider allows for instant adjustments to your stop-loss distance.

### Real-Time Risk Dashboard
Monitor your total market exposure at a glance.
- View total open risk percentage
- Track daily loss exposure against your personal limits
- Identify "Correlation Buckets" to prevent over-exposure in a single asset class

### Professional Trade Journal
Record your trades with more than just numbers.
- Add trade rationale and technical setups
- Tag your emotional state (Greed, Fear, Calm, etc.)
- Search and filter history by symbol or category

### Psychology Insights (Pro)
Master your mind with our unique Emotion Heatmap. See exactly which mental states correlate with your wins and losses. Identify behavioral patterns that are costing you money.

### Advanced Stats & Equity Curve
- Track your account growth with a high-fidelity equity chart
- Monitor Expectancy, Max Drawdown, and Rule Compliance
- Multi-Account Support: Keep your Personal, Demo, and Prop Firm accounts isolated

### Secure Cloud Sync (Pro)
Never lose your data again. Your journal is encrypted and synced across all your Android devices in real-time.

### Built for Prop Firm Traders
Stay compliant with daily loss limits and total drawdown rules. RisCalc is the perfect companion for passing and keeping your funded accounts.
